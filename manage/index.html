How it works

  1. Click Manage subscription above.
  2. Enter the email address you subscribed with. Stripe sends you a secure, single-use link.
  3. Open that link from your inbox to see your subscription and cancel in one click. A cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for.

The link comes from Stripe, our payment processor, so no one else can open your billing page just by knowing your email. If a link expires, request a new one from the same page.

Checking your email or a password on Am I Exposed is always free and has no subscription — this page is only for the optional breach or settlement monitoring plans. If you never started monitoring, there is nothing to cancel.
