Amn Healthcare data breach settlement: how to claim

Amn Healthcare is paying a $4.5 million fund, and the claim window is still open. Claims close October 27, 2026. No proof of purchase is required.

The numbers

Settlement fund$4.5 million
What it coveredTBD
Claim deadlineOctober 27, 2026
CaseCarrero, et al. v. AMN Healthcare Inc., et al., Case No. 37-2023-00052448-CU-NP-CTL, in the Superior Court of the State of California, County of San Diego

Who was covered

Individuals physically present in California whose communications were monitored and/or recorded by AMN Healthcare without consent.

Where to claim

File at www.amncipasettlement.com, the site run by the administrator the court appointed. That is the only place a claim counts, it is free, and nobody should ever charge you to file one.

Source: the settlement administrator

Being in the breach is not the same as being in the class. Class membership usually turns on having received the company’s own notice letter, and only the settlement administrator can confirm it. We are not lawyers, we take no fee from any claim, and we never file on anyone’s behalf.
