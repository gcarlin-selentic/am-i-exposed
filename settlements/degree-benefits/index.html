Degree Benefits data breach settlement

Degree Benefits settled a data breach claim for $990,000, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on November 16, 2023, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$990,000
Maximum per person$5,000
What it covered$5,000
Claim deadline (passed)November 16, 2023
Final approval hearingNovember 16, 2023
CaseFernandez, et al. v. 90 Degree Benefits LLC, et al., Case No. 2:22-cv-00799-SCD, in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin

Who was covered

Individuals who received a notification from 90 Degree Benefits informing them that their personal information and protected health information may have been compromised in data breaches that occurred in February and December 2022.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is 90DBSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
