Absolute Dental data breach settlement

Absolute Dental settled a data breach claim for $3.3 million, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on June 18, 2026, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$3.3 million
Maximum per person$5,000
What it coveredUp to $5,000 in documented losses or a cash payout. California residents can receive a double payment.
Claim deadline (passed)June 18, 2026
Final approval hearingJuly 30, 2026
CaseJordan, et al. v. Absolute Dental Group LLC, et al ., Case No. 2:25-cv-00986-JCM-EJY, in the U.S. District Court for the District of Nevada

Who was covered

The class action settlement benefits individuals whose personal information was potentially compromised in a data breach that occurred between Feb. 19, 2025, and March 5, 2025, and who received a data breach notification from Absolute Dental Group.

What was exposed

  • Social Security Number
  • Date of Birth
  • Health Insurance Information
  • Contact Information
  • Treatment Information
  • Financial Account Information
  • Payment Card Information
  • Diagnosis
  • Health Information
  • Name
  • Medical History
  • Patient ID Number

What is on the record

The incident is recorded as August 2025.

The case was first listed on August 28, 2025. A settlement followed.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is AbsoluteDataSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.

Source: Top Class Actions · ClassAction.org
