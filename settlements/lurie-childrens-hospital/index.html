Lurie Childrens Hospital data breach settlement

Lurie Childrens Hospital settled a data breach claim.

The numbers

What it coveredNon-monetary benefits Increased monitoring of employee access Better protection for highly sensitive data Additional employee training on medical records
Final approval hearingJanuary 25, 2023
CaseDoe v. Lurie Children’s Hospital of Chicago, Case No. 2020-CH-04123, in the Circuit Court of Cook County, Illinois, County Department, Chancery Division

Who was covered

Individuals who received a written notification that their medical records or a family member’s medical records may have been compromised by a Lurie Children’s Hospital employee between 09/10/2018 – 09/22/2019 and 11/01/2018 – 02/29/2020

Where to claim

Claims go through the court-appointed administrator, at DoeLurieSettlement.com. That is the only place a claim counts. Nobody should ever charge you to file one.
