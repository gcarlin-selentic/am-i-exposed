Parker Hannifin data breach settlement

Parker Hannifin settled a data breach claim for $1,750,000. The claim window closed on July 14, 2023, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$1,750,000
What it coveredVaries
Claim deadline (passed)July 14, 2023
Final approval hearingAugust 2, 2023
CaseChristiansen, et al. v. Parker Hannifin Corp., Case No. 1:22-cv-00835-DAP, in the U.S. District Court in the Northern District of Ohio

Who was covered

Current and former Parker Hannifin employees who received a letter notifying them of the March 2022 data breach.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is PHDataSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
