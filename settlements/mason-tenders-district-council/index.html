Mason Tenders District Council data breach settlement

Mason Tenders District Council settled a data breach claim that paid up to $2,950 per person. The claim window closed on October 3, 2023, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Maximum per person$2,950
What it covered$2,950
Claim deadline (passed)October 3, 2023
Final approval hearingOctober 11, 2023
CaseVandermark, et al. v. Mason Tenders’ District Council Welfare Fund, et al. , Index No. 153365/2023, in the Supreme Court of the State of New York, County of New

Who was covered

Those who have received a notification letter from Mason Tenders stating that their personal identifying information and/or personal health information was or may have been compromised in the data incident.
