Ashley Madison data breach settlement

Ashley Madison settled a data breach claim that paid up to $3,500 per person. The claim window closed on January 2, 2018, so this one can no longer be claimed.

Who was covered

Class Members include all persons in the U.S. who used Ashley Madison on or before July 20, 2015. Within that group, those who may be entitled to receive monetary benefits include but aren’t limited to: Those whose personal information was exposed in the data breach; Those who purchased either the Paid Delete or Full Delete option from Ashley Madison; Those who suffered losses because of the data
