Sea Mar data breach settlement

Sea Mar settled a data breach claim for $4.4 million. The claim window closed on December 30, 2022, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$4.4 million
What it coveredVaries
Claim deadline (passed)December 30, 2022
Final approval hearingDecember 9, 2022
CaseJeffrie Allan Summers II v. Sea Mar Community Health Centers , Case No. 2-2-00773-7-SEA in the Superior Court of the State of Washington for King County

Who was covered

The settlement benefits individuals whose private information was compromised in the Sea Mar Community Health Centers data breach that occurred between December 2020 and March 2021.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is CommunityHealthDataIncident.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
