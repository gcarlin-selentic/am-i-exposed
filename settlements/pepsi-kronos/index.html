Pepsi Kronos data breach settlement

Pepsi Kronos settled a data breach claim for $12,750,000.

The numbers

Settlement fund$12,750,000
What it coveredVaries
Final approval hearingApril 4, 2023
CaseStevens, et al. v. PepsiCo. Inc., et al. , Case No. 7-22-cv-00802-NSR, in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York

Who was covered

Current and former Pepsi employees who were impacted by the Kronos outage between Dec. 5, 2021, and April 8, 2022. The settlement benefits two state subclasses of class members from New York or California.
