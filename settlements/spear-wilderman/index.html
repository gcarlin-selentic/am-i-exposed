Spear Wilderman data breach settlement

Spear Wilderman settled a data breach claim for $800,000, with up to $1,625 per person. The claim window closed on November 27, 2023, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$800,000
Maximum per person$1,625
What it covered$1,625
Claim deadline (passed)November 27, 2023
Final approval hearingDecember 20, 2023
CaseRaniell v. Spear Wilderman , Case No. 23-cv-01442, in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania Hassell v. Spear Wilderman , Case No. 230

Who was covered

Individuals whose personal information was actually or potentially compromised during a Spear Wilderman data breach in May 2021
