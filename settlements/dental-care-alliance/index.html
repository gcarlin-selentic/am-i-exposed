Dental Care Alliance data breach settlement

Dental Care Alliance settled a data breach claim for $3 million, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on August 25, 2022, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$3 million
Maximum per person$5,000
What it coveredUp to $5,000 and two free years of Identity Guard
Claim deadline (passed)August 25, 2022
Final approval hearingSeptember 1, 2022
CaseParas v. Dental Care Alliance LLC , Case No. 22-ev-000181, in the State Court of Fulton County, Georgia

Who was covered

The settlement benefits individuals who were notified of the Dental Care Alliance data breach in October 2020, December 2020 or April 2021. This includes a subclass of individuals whose notice indicated their Social Security number, financial account information, bank account data and/or driver’s license number were potentially affected by the data breach.
