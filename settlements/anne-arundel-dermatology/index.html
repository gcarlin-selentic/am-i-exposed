Anne Arundel Dermatology data breach settlement

Anne Arundel Dermatology settled a data breach claim for $2.4 million, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on July 8, 2026, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$2.4 million
Maximum per person$5,000
What it coveredUp to $5,000 in documented losses, or an alternative payout estimated at $100.
Claim deadline (passed)July 8, 2026
Final approval hearingJuly 16, 2026
CaseIn re: Anne Arundel Data Breach Litigation , Case No. 1:25-cv-02274-GLR, in the U.S. District Court for the District of Maryland

Who was covered

All persons in the United States who provided personal information, including but not limited to PII or PHI, to AAD, or about whom AAD otherwise collected, received, or possessed personal information, including but not limited to PII or PHI, on or before Dec. 9, 2026.

What was exposed

  • Date of Birth
  • Medical Information
  • Health Insurance Information
  • Address
  • Name

What is on the record

The incident is recorded as July 2025, and notice went to attorneys general in 1 state.

The case was first listed on July 14, 2025. A settlement followed.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is AnneArundelPrivacySettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
