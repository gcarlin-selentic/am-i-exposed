St Joseph Health System Medical data breach settlement

St Joseph Health System Medical settled a data breach claim that paid up to $241 per person.

The numbers

Maximum per person$241
What it coveredA cash payment of at least $241.35. Class Members may also file a claim to seek reimbursement of certain identity theft losses and out-of-pocket expenses related to the data breach.
Final approval hearingFebruary 3, 2016
CaseSt. Joseph Health System Medical Information Cases , Case No. JCCP 4716, in the Superior Court of the State of California, County of Orange

Who was covered

Class Members of the data breach settlement include all patients of St. Joseph Health System whose confidential medical information was made publicly accessible on the internet by St. Joseph at any time between Feb. 1, 2011 and Feb. 28, 2012.

Where to claim

Claims go through the court-appointed administrator, at www.SJHSDataBreachClassAction.com. That is the only place a claim counts. Nobody should ever charge you to file one.

Being in the breach is not the same as being in the class. Class membership usually turns on having received the company’s own notice letter, and only the settlement administrator can confirm it. We are not lawyers, we take no fee from any claim, and we never file on anyone’s behalf.
