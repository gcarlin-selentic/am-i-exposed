Lakeview Loan Servicing data breach settlement

Lakeview Loan Servicing settled a data breach claim for $26 million, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on June 22, 2026, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$26 million
Maximum per person$5,000
What it coveredUp to $5,000 for out-of-pocket losses, a pro rata cash payment and an additional pro rata payment for California residents.
Claim deadline (passed)June 22, 2026
Final approval hearingJuly 2, 2026
CaseIn re: Lakeview Loan Servicing Data Breach Litigation, Case No. 1:22-cv-20955-GAYLES, in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Florida Lazarus v.

Who was covered

Individuals, including a California subclass, who received a data breach notice from Lakeview Loan Servicing informing them that their personally identifiable information may have been compromised in a data breach on Oct. 11, 2021.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is LakeviewDataBreachSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
