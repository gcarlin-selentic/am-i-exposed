Minted data breach settlement

Minted settled a data breach claim for $5 million, with up to $43 per person. The claim window closed on September 16, 2021, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$5 million
Maximum per person$43
What it coveredAbout $43.
Claim deadline (passed)September 16, 2021
Final approval hearingDecember 2, 2021
CaseAtkinson, et al. v. Minted Inc. , Case No. 3:20-cv-03869-VC in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California

Who was covered

Any United States resident with a Minted account, or those who provided Minted with their name, email and street addresses, or other personal information via email, the Minted website, or other online communication on or before June 27, 2020.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is MintedSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
