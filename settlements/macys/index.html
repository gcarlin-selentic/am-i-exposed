Macys data breach settlement

Macys settled a data breach claim that paid up to $1,500 per person. The claim window closed on April 6, 2020, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Maximum per person$1,500
What it coveredUp to $1,500. The Macy’s data breach class action settlement notes that two types of payments are available to Class Members who submit valid Claim Forms, including the following: Reimbursement for documented expense related to the cyber attack, up to $1,500 Reimbursement for undocumented time spent attempting to recover from the cyber attack, up to 2 hours at $15/hr, for a total of $30
Claim deadline (passed)April 6, 2020
Final approval hearingJune 2, 2020
CaseAnna Carroll v. Macy’s Inc., et al., Case 2:18-cv-01060-RDP, in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Alabama

Who was covered

Class Members eligible for the Macy’s data breach class action settlement include customers who live in the United States and received a notification in July 2018 from Macy’s regarding unauthorized activity that resulted from the cyber attack.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is www.MCOMsettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
