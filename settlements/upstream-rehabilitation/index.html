Upstream Rehabilitation data breach settlement

Upstream Rehabilitation settled a data breach claim for $4.3 million, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on January 30, 2025, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$4.3 million
Maximum per person$5,000
What it covered$5,000
Claim deadline (passed)January 30, 2025
Final approval hearingFebruary 24, 2025
CaseJeremy Hufstetler, et al. v. Upstream Rehabilitation Inc., et al. , Case No. 01-CV-2024-902563.00, in the Tenth Judicial Circuit Court of Jefferson County, Alab

Who was covered

Individuals whose personal health information and/or personally identifiable information was exposed to unauthorized third parties as a result of a data breach that occurred between Jan. 24 and Jan. 31, 2023, and between Feb. 3 and Feb. 9, 2023

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is UpstreamDataSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
