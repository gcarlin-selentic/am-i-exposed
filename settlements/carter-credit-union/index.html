Carter Credit Union data breach settlement: how to claim

Carter Credit Union is paying up to $2,500 per person, and the claim window is still open. Claims close November 19, 2026.

The numbers

Maximum per person$2,500
What it coveredUp to $2,500 for documented losses or a $50 cash payment
Claim deadlineNovember 19, 2026
CaseEllis, et al. v. Carter Credit Union d/b/a Carter Federal Credit Union, Case No. 662980-C, in the 1st Judicial District Court for the Parish of Caddo, State of

Who was covered

Individuals whose personal information was compromised in the Carter Credit Union data breach that occurred on or around June 25, 2025.

Where to claim

File at ccudatasettlement.com, the site run by the administrator the court appointed. That is the only place a claim counts, it is free, and nobody should ever charge you to file one.

Source: the settlement administrator

Being in the breach is not the same as being in the class. Class membership usually turns on having received the company’s own notice letter, and only the settlement administrator can confirm it. We are not lawyers, we take no fee from any claim, and we never file on anyone’s behalf.
