Tampa General Hospital data breach settlement

Tampa General Hospital settled a data breach claim for $6.8 million, with up to $7,500 per person. The claim window closed on January 12, 2025, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$6.8 million
Maximum per person$7,500
What it coveredUp to $7,500
Claim deadline (passed)January 12, 2025
Final approval hearingFebruary 3, 2025
CaseDiPierro, et al. v. Florida Health Sciences Center Inc. d/b/a Tampa General Hospital , Case No. 23-CA-013984, in the 13th Judicial Circuit Court for Hillsboroug

Who was covered

Individuals whom Tampa General Hospital notified that their private information was potentially compromised in a data security incident that occurred between May 12, 2023, and May 30, 2023

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is FloridaHealthSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
