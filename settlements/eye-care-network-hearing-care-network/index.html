Eye Care Network Hearing Care Network data breach settlement

Eye Care Network Hearing Care Network settled a data breach claim for $3 million, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on May 1, 2023, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$3 million
Maximum per person$5,000
What it covered$5,000
Claim deadline (passed)May 1, 2023
Final approval hearingJune 22, 2023
CaseDesue, et al. v. 20/20 Eye Care Network Inc., et al., Case No. 0:21-cv-61275-RAR, in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Florida

Who was covered

Individuals who received a data breach notification from 20/20 Eye Care Network or 20/20 Hearing Care Network as a result of the data breach which occurred in January 2021
