Urology Center Of Colorado data breach settlement

Urology Center Of Colorado settled a data breach claim. The claim window closed on November 7, 2022, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

What it coveredVaries
Claim deadline (passed)November 7, 2022
Final approval hearingOctober 26, 2022
CaseSnyder et al. v. The Urology Center of Colorado, P.C., Case No. 2021CV33707, in the 2nd District Court, County of Denver, State of Colorado

Who was covered

The settlement benefits individuals who received a notice that their personal identifying information may have been compromised in an Urology Center of Colorado data breach between Sept. 7 and 8, 2021. There are 137,820 people who received a data breach notice.
