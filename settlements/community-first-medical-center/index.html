Community First Medical Center data breach settlement

Community First Medical Center settled a data breach claim for $1 million, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on April 2, 2026, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$1 million
Maximum per person$5,000
What it coveredUp to $5,000 in out-of-pocket expenses or a cash payment of around $40.
Claim deadline (passed)April 2, 2026
Final approval hearingMarch 25, 2026
CasePacheco, et al. v. Community First Healthcare of Illinois Inc. d/b/a Community First Medical Center , Case No. 2023CH08487, in the Circuit Court of Cook County,

Who was covered

Individuals whose personal information was potentially compromised in the July 12, 2023, Community First Medical Center data breach or those who received a data breach notification from the defendant.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is CFMCSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
