Norton Healthcare data breach settlement

Norton Healthcare settled a data breach claim for $11 million, with up to $2,500 per person. The claim window closed on May 18, 2026, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$11 million
Maximum per person$2,500
What it coveredUp to $2,500 for out-of-pocket losses incl. $80 for lost time, or a cash payment of at least $5.
Claim deadline (passed)May 18, 2026
Final approval hearingMay 15, 2026
CaseBerthold, et al. v. Norton Healthcare Inc., et al. , Case No. 23-CI-003349, in the Jefferson Circuit Court, Division Two, Kentucky

Who was covered

Individuals who received a letter from the defendants notifying them that their personal information may have been compromised in the data incident.

What was exposed

  • Social Security Number
  • Date of Birth
  • Full Name
  • Financial Account Number
  • Driver's License Number
  • Government-Issued ID Number
  • Contact Information
  • Insurance Information
  • Health Information
  • Medical Identification Numbers
  • Digital Signatures

What is on the record

The incident is recorded as December 2023.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is NortonDataIncidentSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
