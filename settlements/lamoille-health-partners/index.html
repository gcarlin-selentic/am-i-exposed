Lamoille Health Partners data breach settlement

Lamoille Health Partners settled a data breach claim for $540,000, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on June 20, 2024, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$540,000
Maximum per person$5,000
What it covered$5,000
Claim deadline (passed)June 20, 2024
Final approval hearingSeptember 30, 2024
CaseMarshall v. Lamoille Health Partners Inc., Case No. 2:22-cv-00166-wks, in the U.S. District Court for the District of Vermont

Who was covered

Individuals whose personal information was accessed or potentially compromised in the Lamoille Health Partners data breach in August 2022
