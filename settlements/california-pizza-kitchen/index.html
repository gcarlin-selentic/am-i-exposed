California Pizza Kitchen data breach settlement

California Pizza Kitchen settled a data breach claim. The claim window closed on October 27, 2022, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

What it coveredVaries
Claim deadline (passed)October 27, 2022
Final approval hearingNovember 3, 2022
CaseCalifornia Pizza Kitchen Data Breach Litigation , Master File No. 8:21-cv-01928-DOC-KES, in the U.S. District Court for the Central District of California

Who was covered

People whose information was compromised in the California Pizza Kitchen data breach announced in November 2021 and who received a notice regarding the breach. The settlement includes a subclass of California consumers.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is CPKDataBreachSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.

Source: Top Class Actions

Being in the breach is not the same as being in the class. Class membership usually turns on having received the company’s own notice letter, and only the settlement administrator can confirm it. We are not lawyers, we take no fee from any claim, and we never file on anyone’s behalf.
