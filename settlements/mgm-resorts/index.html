Mgm Resorts data breach settlement

Mgm Resorts settled a data breach claim for $45 million, with up to $15,000 per person. The claim window closed on June 3, 2025, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$45 million
Maximum per person$15,000
What it coveredVaries. Payments vary according to the type of data compromised plus one year’s monitoring of financial accounts. Reimbursable expenses are up to $15,000.
Claim deadline (passed)June 3, 2025
Final approval hearingJune 18, 2025
CaseIn re: MGM International Resorts Data Breach Litigation, Case No. 2:20-cv-00376-GMN-NJK, and Tanya Owens, et al. v. MGM Resorts International, et al., Case No.

Who was covered

The MGM Resorts settlement benefits individuals whose personal information was compromised in July 2019 and September 2023 data breaches and who received a notice from MGM informing them of their inclusion in the settlement.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is MGMdataSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
