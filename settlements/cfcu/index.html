Cfcu data breach settlement

Cfcu settled a data breach claim for $2.9 million, with up to $7,500 per person. The claim window closed on December 22, 2025, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$2.9 million
Maximum per person$7,500
What it coveredUp to $7,500 for documented out-of-pocket losses or a pro rata payment estimated at $125.
Claim deadline (passed)December 22, 2025
Final approval hearingJanuary 7, 2026
CaseIn re: Communication Federal Credit Union Data Breach Litigation , Case No. CJ-2024-5388, in the District Court for Oklahoma County, Oklahoma

Who was covered

All residents of the United States whose personal information was potentially exposed in the Communication Federal Credit Union data breach from Dec. 31, 2023, to Jan. 11, 2024, including those who received a data breach notification letter.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is CFCUDataIncidentSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
