Kemper Corp Infinity Insurance data breach settlement

Kemper Corp Infinity Insurance settled a data breach claim. The claim window closed on March 15, 2022, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

What it coveredVaries
Claim deadline (passed)March 15, 2022
Final approval hearingMarch 15, 2022
CaseAguallo, et al. v. Kemper Corp. and Infinity Insurance Co. Inc. , Case No. 1:21-cv-01883 in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, Easte

Who was covered

Class Members include U.S. citizens who were affected by a data incident involving the Kemper Corp. and Infinity Insurance Co. that occurred Dec. 14, 2020, and March 25, 2021, and who received notifications by mail that explained their information was compromised during the incident. There is also a Subclass for California residents.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is InfinityClassSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
