Cardiovascular Consultants data breach settlement

Cardiovascular Consultants settled a data breach claim for $3,850,000, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on July 1, 2026, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$3,850,000
Maximum per person$5,000
What it coveredUp to $5,000 in documented losses or a pro rata payment of $75
Claim deadline (passed)July 1, 2026
Final approval hearingAugust 18, 2026
CaseStroup, et al. v. Cardiovascular Consultants Ltd. , Case No. CV2023-020048, in the Superior Court of Arizona, Maricopa County

Who was covered

The class action settlement benefits i ndividuals residing in the United States whose personal information was potentially compromised in the Cardiovascular Consultants data breach discovered in September 2023, including those who received notice of the data breach.

What was exposed

  • Social Security Number
  • Date of Birth
  • Full Name
  • Medical Information
  • Driver's License Number
  • Address
  • Contact Information
  • Treatment Information
  • Billing Information
  • Policy Number
  • Group Number
  • Diagnosis

What is on the record

The incident is recorded as December 2023.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is www.CVCDataSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.

Source: Top Class Actions · ClassAction.org
