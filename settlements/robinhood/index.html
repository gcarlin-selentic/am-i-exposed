Robinhood data breach settlement

Robinhood settled a data breach claim that paid up to $260 per person. The claim window closed on January 17, 2023, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Maximum per person$260
What it coveredUp to $260
Claim deadline (passed)January 17, 2023
Final approval hearingMay 16, 2023
CaseSiddharth Mehta, Kevin Qian, and Michael Furtado v. Robinhood Financial LLC and Robinhood Securities, LLC , Case No. 5:21-cv-01013-SVK, in the United States Dis

Who was covered

The settlement benefits individuals who experienced an unauthorized access incident on their Robinhood account between Jan. 1, 2020, and April 27, 2022, that was either reported to Robinhood by customers or reported to customers by Robinhood.
