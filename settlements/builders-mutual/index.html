Builders Mutual data breach settlement

Builders Mutual settled a data breach claim for $1,475,000, with up to $10,000 per person. The claim window closed on January 8, 2025, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$1,475,000
Maximum per person$10,000
What it covered$10,000
Claim deadline (passed)January 8, 2025
Final approval hearingJanuary 17, 2025
CaseIn re: Builders Mutual Data Security Incident Litigation , Case No. 5:23-CV-579, in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina

Who was covered

Individuals whose private information the Builders Mutual data breach compromised in December 2022

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is BuildersMutualDataBreach.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
