Cafepress data breach settlement

Cafepress settled a data breach claim for $500,000. The claim window closed on March 10, 2024, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$500,000
What it coveredTBD
Claim deadline (passed)March 10, 2024
Final approval hearingJune 24, 2022
CaseIn the Matter of Residual Pumpkin Entity LLC, a limited liability company, formerly d/b/a CafePress; and PlanetArt LLC, a limited liability company, d/b/a CafeP

Who was covered

Consumers whose Social Security numbers were exposed in a CafePress data breach in 2019.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is FTC.gov/enforcement/refunds/cafepress-settlement. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
