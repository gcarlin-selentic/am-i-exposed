Christies data breach settlement

Christies settled a data breach claim for $990,000, with up to $10,000 per person. The claim window closed on June 19, 2025, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$990,000
Maximum per person$10,000
What it coveredUp to $10,000 for documented monetary losses and a pro rata cash payment currently estimated at $100. California class members may also receive an additional $100 statutory payment.
Claim deadline (passed)June 19, 2025
Final approval hearingJuly 22, 2025
CaseIn re Christie’s Data Breach Litigation , Case No. 24-cv-4221 (JMF), in the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York

Who was covered

Individuals who reside in the United States whose private information was compromised by the Christie’s data breach in May 2024 and who were sent notice of the data breach.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is ChristiesDataSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
