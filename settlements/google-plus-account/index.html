Google Plus Account data breach settlement

Google Plus Account settled a data breach claim that paid up to $12 per person. The claim window closed on October 8, 2020, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Maximum per person$12
What it coveredUp to $12. According to the Google+ class action settlement, Class Members who file a valid claim form will receive a pro rata share of the fund starting with up to a $5 payment. However, if settlement funds remain after the initial $5 payout, an additional distribution of up to $12 (total) will be paid. * The settlement also allows for a reduced payout of lower than $5 if the number of claimants
Claim deadline (passed)October 8, 2020
Final approval hearingJanuary 7, 2021
CaseIn re: Google Plus Profile Litigation , Case No. 5:18-cv-06164, in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California

Who was covered

Class Members include all U.S. residents who had a Google+ account between Jan. 1, 2015 and April 2, 2019 and had their “non-public Profile Information exposed as a result of the software bugs Google announced on October 8, 2018 and December 10, 2018.”

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is GooglePlusDataLitigation.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
