University Of Phoenix data breach settlement: how to claim

University Of Phoenix is paying up to $25 per person, and the claim window is still open. Claims close November 5, 2026.

The numbers

Settlement fund$8,750,000
Maximum per person$25
What it covered$25
Claim deadlineNovember 5, 2026
CaseBrand, et al. v. The University of Phoenix Inc., Case No. 2026LA000170, in the Circuit Court for Sangamon County, Seventh Judicial Circuit, Springfield, Illinoi

Who was covered

Individuals enrolled at the University of Phoenix between April 1, 2023, and Oct. 6, 2026, or who were enrolled in Illinois during the same period.

Where to claim

File at uoppixelsettlement.com, the site run by the administrator the court appointed. That is the only place a claim counts, it is free, and nobody should ever charge you to file one.
