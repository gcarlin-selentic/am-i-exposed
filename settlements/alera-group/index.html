Alera Group data breach settlement

Alera Group settled a data breach claim for $2 million, with up to $3,500 per person. The claim window closed on June 29, 2026, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$2 million
Maximum per person$3,500
What it coveredUp to $3,500 in documented losses or an alternative cash payment of around $50
Claim deadline (passed)June 29, 2026
Final approval hearingAugust 3, 2026
CaseSophia Grubb, et al. v. Alera Group Inc ., Case No. CACE25019102, in the Circuit Court for Broward County, Florida

Who was covered

The Alera Group class action settlement benefits all individuals residing in the United States who received notice that their private information may have been compromised in the 2024 data breach.

What was exposed

  • Social Security Number
  • Date of Birth
  • Medical Information
  • Address
  • Financial Account Information
  • Demographic Information
  • Medical Record Number
  • Biometric Data
  • Username and Password
  • Birth Certificate
  • Credit Card Information
  • Electronic Signature

What is on the record

The incident is recorded as May 2025, and about 10,874 people were affected.

The case was first listed on May 22, 2025. A settlement followed.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is AleraGroupDataSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.

Source: Top Class Actions · ClassAction.org
