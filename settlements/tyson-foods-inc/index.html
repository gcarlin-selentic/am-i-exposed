Tyson Foods Inc class action settlement: how to claim

Tyson Foods Inc is paying a $82.5 million fund, and the claim window is still open. Claims close November 30, 2026.

The numbers

Settlement fund$82.5 million
What it coveredPro rata payment based on qualifying purchases
Claim deadlineNovember 30, 2026
CaseTyson Beef Class Action Settlement

Who was covered

Individuals and entities who purchased boxed or case-ready beef directly from Tyson or other defendants between 2015 and 2020 may be eligible.

Where to claim

File at www.beefdirectpurchasersettlement.com/file, the site run by the administrator the court appointed. That is the only place a claim counts, it is free, and nobody should ever charge you to file one.

Source: the settlement administrator

Being in the breach is not the same as being in the class. Class membership usually turns on having received the company’s own notice letter, and only the settlement administrator can confirm it. We are not lawyers, we take no fee from any claim, and we never file on anyone’s behalf.
