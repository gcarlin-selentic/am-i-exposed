Somnia data breach settlement

Somnia settled a data breach claim for $2,425,000, with up to $2,500 per person. The claim window closed on January 2, 2025, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$2,425,000
Maximum per person$2,500
What it coveredUp to $2,500
Claim deadline (passed)January 2, 2025
Final approval hearingApril 28, 2025
CaseChabak, et al. v. Somnia Inc., et al. , Case No. 7:22-cv-09341-PMH, in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York

Who was covered

Individuals whose personal information the Somnia data breach compromised in on July 11, 2022, including those who received anesthesiology services from the following providers: Resource Anesthesiology Associates of NY NY Anesthesia Services PC Resource Anesthesia Associates of NC DB RAA of WA Somnia Pain Mgt of PA PC Resource Anesthesiology Associates of NJ LLC CA Outpatient Anesthesia Services P

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is SomniaSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
