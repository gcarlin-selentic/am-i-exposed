The Container Store class action settlement: how to claim

The Container Store is paying up to $75 per person, and the claim window is still open. Claims close December 20, 2026.

The numbers

Settlement fund$780,000
Maximum per person$75
What it covered$75
Claim deadlineDecember 20, 2026
CaseCadigan v. The Container Store Inc.

Who was covered

Individuals who are legally blind and made purchases in Massachusetts.

Where to claim

File at www.tcssettlement.com/Login, the site run by the administrator the court appointed. That is the only place a claim counts, it is free, and nobody should ever charge you to file one.

Source: the settlement administrator

Being in the breach is not the same as being in the class. Class membership usually turns on having received the company’s own notice letter, and only the settlement administrator can confirm it. We are not lawyers, we take no fee from any claim, and we never file on anyone’s behalf.
