Hudsons Bay Company Saks data breach settlement

Hudsons Bay Company Saks settled a data breach claim for $2 million, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on January 31, 2022, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$2 million
Maximum per person$5,000
What it coveredUp to $5,000
Claim deadline (passed)January 31, 2022
Final approval hearingMarch 1, 2022
CaseIn re: Hudson’s Bay Company Data Security Incident Consumer Litigation , Case No. 1:18-cv-08472 in the Southern District of New York

Who was covered

The Class includes anyone who used a credit, debit, or prepaid card (other than a Saks First branded credit card) at a Saks location, Lord & Taylor store, or Saks OFF 5TH between May 1, 2017, and April 1, 2018, within the United States and its territories.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is HBCSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
