Sequoia Benefits And Insurance data breach settlement

Sequoia Benefits And Insurance settled a data breach claim for $8.7 million, with up to $7,500 per person. The claim window closed on March 11, 2026, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$8.7 million
Maximum per person$7,500
What it coveredUp to $7,500 for documented losses or a $75 payout and an additional $150 for California residents.
Claim deadline (passed)March 11, 2026
Final approval hearingApril 7, 2026
CaseIn re: Sequoia Benefits and Insurance Data Breach Litigation , Case No. 22-cv-08217-RFL, in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California

Who was covered

Individuals who received a data breach notification from Sequoia informing them their personal information may have been compromised in a data breach between Sept. 22 and Oct. 6, 2022, or individuals who believe they may have been affected by the data breach.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is SequoiaDataSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
