Cencora data breach settlement

Cencora settled a data breach claim for $40 million, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on January 19, 2026, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$40 million
Maximum per person$5,000
What it coveredUp to $5,000 in documented losses or a pro rata cash payment.
Claim deadline (passed)January 19, 2026
Final approval hearingFebruary 5, 2026
CaseAnaya, et al. v. Cencora Inc., et al ., Case No. 2:24-cv-02961-CMR, in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania

Who was covered

The settlement benefits individuals and entities whose personal information was involved in the Cencora data breach and who received mailed notice of the incident or substitute notice through Cencora’s website and/or a media press release or who were on “inquiry notice” because of circumstances occurring on or about Feb. 27, 2024, suggesting that they may be aware of harm potentially arising from

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is CencoraIncidentSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
