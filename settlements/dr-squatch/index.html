Dr Squatch class action settlement: how to claim

Dr Squatch is paying up to $12 per person, and the claim window is still open. Claims close November 27, 2026.

The numbers

Settlement fund$9 million
Maximum per person$12
What it coveredUp to $12.50
Claim deadlineNovember 27, 2026
CaseGuzman, et al. v. Dr. Squatch LLC, Case No. 25STCV03523, in the Superior Court of California for the County of Los Angeles

Who was covered

Consumers who purchased Dr. Squatch products labeled 'natural' between Nov. 1, 2018, and Aug. 29, 2026.

Where to claim

File at personalcareproductssettlement.com, the site run by the administrator the court appointed. That is the only place a claim counts, it is free, and nobody should ever charge you to file one.

Source: the settlement administrator

Being in the breach is not the same as being in the class. Class membership usually turns on having received the company’s own notice letter, and only the settlement administrator can confirm it. We are not lawyers, we take no fee from any claim, and we never file on anyone’s behalf.
