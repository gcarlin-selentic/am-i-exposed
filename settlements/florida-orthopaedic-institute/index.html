Florida Orthopaedic Institute data breach settlement

Florida Orthopaedic Institute settled a data breach claim for $4 million. The claim window closed on September 16, 2022, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$4 million
What it coveredCash payment and identity theft protection services.
Claim deadline (passed)September 16, 2022
Final approval hearingSeptember 29, 2022
CaseStoll et al. v. Musculoskeletal Institute , Case No. 8:20-cv-01798-CEH-AAS, in the U.S. District Court for the Middle District of Florida

Who was covered

The settlement benefits current and former patients of the Florida Orthopaedic Institute who were sent a letter in June 2020 notifying them that their information may have been compromised as part of a data breach.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is FLOrthoSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
