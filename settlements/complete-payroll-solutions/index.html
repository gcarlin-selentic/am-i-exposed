Complete Payroll Solutions data breach settlement

Complete Payroll Solutions settled a data breach claim for $2.6 million, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on June 18, 2026, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$2.6 million
Maximum per person$5,000
What it coveredUp to $5,000 for documented losses, an estimated $100 payout and three years of credit monitoring.
Claim deadline (passed)June 18, 2026
Final approval hearingJune 25, 2026
CaseDunn, et al. v. Complete Payroll Solutions LLC , Case No. 1:25-cv-30045-LTS, in the U.S. District Court for the District of Massachusetts

Who was covered

The class action settlement benefits individuals who received a notice from Complete Payroll Solutions regarding potential impact from a data breach discovered in March 2024 or who were otherwise determined to have potentially had their personal information compromised by the incident.

What was exposed

  • Social Security Number
  • Financial Information
  • Health Insurance Information
  • Driver's License Number
  • Address

What is on the record

The incident is recorded as April 2025, and the breach was reported nationally.

The case was first listed on April 29, 2025. A settlement followed.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is CPSSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
