Data breach settlements and lawsuits

1,064 cases: 757 that reached a settlement and 307 still in court. Who was covered, how much was paid, and the administrator’s own claim site where one exists.

When a company loses your data, what often follows is not a fine but a settlement, paid to the people whose data it was. The money is real and most of it goes unclaimed, because the notice arrives by post and looks like junk mail. Research out of Duke puts claim rates routinely below 10 percent and often far below 1 percent.

Being in the breach is not the same as being in the class. Class membership usually turns on having received the company’s own notice letter, and only the settlement administrator can confirm it. We are not lawyers, we take no fee from any claim, and we never file on anyone’s behalf.
