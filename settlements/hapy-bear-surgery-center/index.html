Hapy Bear Surgery Center data breach settlement

Hapy Bear Surgery Center settled a data breach claim that paid up to $8,050 per person. The claim window closed on February 4, 2025, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Maximum per person$8,050
What it covered$8,050
Claim deadline (passed)February 4, 2025
Final approval hearingFebruary 24, 2024
CaseIn re: Hapy Bear Surgery Center Data Security Incident Litigation , Case No. VCU307987, in the California Superior Court for Tulare County

Who was covered

Individuals whose personal information was involved in the 2023 data security incident discovered on or about Dec. 27, 2023, including those who received a notice of the data breach

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is HBSCDataSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
