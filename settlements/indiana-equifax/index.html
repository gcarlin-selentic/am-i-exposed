Indiana Equifax data breach settlement

Indiana Equifax settled a data breach claim. The claim window closed on December 16, 2020, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

What it coveredTBD. Under the terms of the settlement, the $19.5 million settlement fund will be used to cover the costs of the litigation and payments to consumers. The net settlement fund will reportedly be distributed evenly amongst Class Members who file a valid claim. Exact payment amounts will depend on the number of valid claim forms filed.
Claim deadline (passed)December 16, 2020
CaseState of Indiana v. Equifax Information Services LLC, et al., Case No. 49-D01-1905-PL-018398, in the Superior Court for Marion County, Indiana

Who was covered

Individuals who were Indiana residents between March 2017 and July 2017 and whose information was affected by the Equifax data breach.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is www.IndianaEquifaxClaims.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
