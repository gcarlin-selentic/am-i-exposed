Schnucks data breach settlement

Schnucks settled a data breach claim that paid up to $10,000 per person. The claim window closed on December 31, 2014, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Maximum per person$10,000
What it coveredThree types of payments are available from the Schnucks class action settlement: Out-of-pocket expenses and lost time (up to $200); $10 payments per card for those who had fraudulent charges that were later reversed or credited back to the credit or debit card account; and Reimbursement of extraordinary unreimbursed monetary losses (up to $10,000) Class Members may submit a claim for all three typ
Claim deadline (passed)December 31, 2014
Final approval hearingJanuary 13, 2015
CaseMcGann, et al. v. Schnuck Markets Inc. , Case No. 1322-CC00800, Division 2, in the Circuit Court of the City of St. Louis, State of Missouri

Who was covered

Class Members of the Schnucks data breach settlement include anyone who used a credit or debit card at a Schnucks store between Dec. 9, 2012 and March 30, 2013. A list of Schnucks stores that have potentially been affected by the data breach is available here .

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is www.SchnucksCardClaims.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
