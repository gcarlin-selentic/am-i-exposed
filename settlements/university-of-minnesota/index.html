University Of Minnesota data breach settlement

University Of Minnesota settled a data breach claim for $5 million, with up to $30 per person. The claim window closed on December 24, 2025, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$5 million
Maximum per person$30
What it covered$30 cash payment and/or 24 months of dark web monitoring.
Claim deadline (passed)December 24, 2025
Final approval hearingJanuary 28, 2026
CaseIn re: Regents of the University of Minnesota Data Litigation , Case No. 27-CV-23-14056, in the Fourth Judicial District Court of the State of Minnesota

Who was covered

Individuals whose personal information was maintained in or accessible via the University of Minnesota’s Legacy Data Warehouse as of Aug. 10, 2021. This includes individuals who submitted information to the university as a prospective student, attended the university as a student, worked at the university as an employee or participated in university programs between 1989 and Aug. 10, 2021.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is UofMDataSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
