Dupage Medical Group data breach settlement

Dupage Medical Group settled a data breach claim for $3 million. The claim window closed on November 29, 2022, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$3 million
What it coveredVaries
Claim deadline (passed)November 29, 2022
Final approval hearingNovember 14, 2022
CaseHestrup et al. v. DuPage Medical Group, Ltd. d/b/a DuPage Medical Group , Case No. 2021L937, in the Circuit Court of the Eighteenth Judicial Circuit Dupage Coun

Who was covered

The settlement benefits individuals whose personal information was compromised in the DuPage Medical Group data breach between July 12 and 13, 2021.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is DMGSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
