Hy Vee data breach settlement

Hy Vee settled a data breach claim for $5,000, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on June 22, 2021, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$5,000
Maximum per person$5,000
What it coveredUp to $5,000 . Claim deadline (passed)June 22, 2021
Final approval hearingJuly 19, 2021
CaseNoreen Perdue, et al. v. Hy-Vee Inc. , Case No. 1:19-cv-01330-MMM-JEH in the U.S. District Court for the Central District of Illinois

Who was covered

Anyone residing in the United States who used a payment card to make a purchase at one of the affected Hy-Vee point-of-sale devices (fuel pumps, drive-thru coffee shops, or restaurants) — generally between Dec. 14, 2018, and Aug. 2, 2019 — at one of the designated locations .

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is GrocerySecurityIncidentSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
