United Health Centers Of San Joaquin Valley data breach settlement

United Health Centers Of San Joaquin Valley settled a data breach claim. The claim window closed on November 19, 2022, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

What it coveredVaries
Claim deadline (passed)November 19, 2022
Final approval hearingFebruary 8, 2023
CaseAvetisyan v. United Health Centers of San Joaquin Valley, Case No. 22-CEG-285, in the Fresno County Superior Court

Who was covered

The settlement benefits individuals whose personal information, including health information, was compromised by unauthorized users in the United Health Centers of San Joaquin Valley data breach Aug. 28, 2021.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is UHCofSJVDataBreach.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
