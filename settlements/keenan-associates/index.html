Keenan Associates data breach settlement

Keenan Associates settled a data breach claim for $33.8 million, with up to $10,000 per person. The claim window closed on October 30, 2025, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$33.8 million
Maximum per person$10,000
What it coveredUp to $10,000 for reimbursement of certain documented losses or a pro rata cash payment from the settlement fund.
Claim deadline (passed)October 30, 2025
Final approval hearingNovember 14, 2025
CaseHeath, et al. v. Keenan & Associates , Case No. 24STCV03018, in the Superior Court of the State of California for the County of Los Angeles

Who was covered

Individuals who were notified by Keenan and Associates that their personal information may have been compromised in a data breach that occurred between Aug. 21, 2023, and Aug. 27, 2023.

What was exposed

  • Social Security Number
  • Full Name
  • Health Insurance Information
  • Driver's License Number
  • Employment Information
  • Health Information
  • Passport Number

What is on the record

The incident is recorded as December 2023.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is KeenanBreachSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.

Source: Top Class Actions · ClassAction.org
