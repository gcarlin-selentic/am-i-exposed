Eureka Casino data breach settlement

Eureka Casino settled a data breach claim for $1 million, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on May 11, 2026, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$1 million
Maximum per person$5,000
What it coveredUp to $5,000 in documented expenses, an alternative pro rata cash payout and an estimated $100 to California residents at the time of the data breach.
Claim deadline (passed)May 11, 2026
Final approval hearingJune 10, 2026
CaseIn re: Eureka Casino Breach Litigation , Case No. 2:23-cv-00276-CDS-NJK, in the United States District Court for the District of Nevada

Who was covered

Individuals whose personal information was compromised in the Eureka Casino data breach that occurred between Nov. 9 and 13, 2022.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is ECHDataSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
