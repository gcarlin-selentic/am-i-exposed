Essen Medical Associates data breach settlement

Essen Medical Associates settled a data breach claim for $4 million, with up to $5,000 per person. The claim window closed on June 1, 2026, so this one can no longer be claimed.

The numbers

Settlement fund$4 million
Maximum per person$5,000
What it coveredUp to $5,000 for documented losses or a cash payment of up to $100.
Claim deadline (passed)June 1, 2026
Final approval hearingJuly 7, 2026
CaseRivera, et al. v. Essen Medical Associates P.C ., Case No. 801239/2024E, in the Supreme Court of the State of New York, Bronx County

Who was covered

U.S. residents whose personal information was potentially compromised in the Essen Medical data breach in March 2023 and who received a mailed notice from Essen informing them of the breach.

Where to claim

The administrator’s site is EHCSettlement.com. The window is closed, so it is there for the record, not to file.
